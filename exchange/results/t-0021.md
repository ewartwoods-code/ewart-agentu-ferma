## 1. Amazon PPC

**Question:** How should EWART WOODS structure and optimize Sponsored Products/Brands campaigns now, while protecting the owner’s narrow-keyword, profit-first playbook?

### Verified/current findings

1. **Amazon currently documents three manual match types: broad, phrase, exact.** Broad can match related terms/synonyms even when the keyword itself is absent; phrase is more restrictive; exact is most restrictive but still includes close variations such as plurals, misspellings, acronyms, and translations. Amazon recommends starting with broad, then using keyword/targeting reports to identify winners and refining bids/keyword groups. **Truth 10/10.**
2. **The “+” broad-match modifier is documented under Sponsored Brands, not Sponsored Products.** Amazon’s current example says `+kids shoes` requires “kids” to appear, while it may match “kids sneakers.” It is not valid evidence for carrying the old `+wood +vase` tactic into Sponsored Products broad campaigns. **Truth 10/10.**
3. **Sponsored Products automatic campaigns use targeting groups rather than manual match types:** close match, loose match, substitutes, complements. These groups can be bid-adjusted or switched off. **Truth 10/10.**
4. **Negative phrase and negative exact are both available.** Amazon documents maximums of 4 words/80 characters for negative phrase and 10 words/80 characters for negative exact. **Truth 10/10.**
5. **Campaign match types cannot be changed after campaign creation, but new keywords with other match types can be added while a campaign runs.** This makes a controlled discovery → harvesting → dedicated-exact workflow practical. **Truth 10/10.**

### Actionable EWART WOODS application

- Keep the owner’s low-budget discovery and proven-keyword concentration. Use Amazon’s actual search-term/targeting reports to harvest converting terms into separate phrase/exact campaigns; add the harvested exact as a negative exact in discovery campaigns only after checking overlap.
- Correct the owner playbook: reserve `+` modifiers for Sponsored Brands unless Amazon’s account UI/help explicitly confirms a current Sponsored Products option. Do not implement `+wood +vase` in SP broad based on the old note.
- Maintain separate auto targeting-group controls. Test close/loose/substitutes/complements separately enough to see which group produces relevant sales; negate irrelevant terms/products rather than making broad bids blindly.
- Keep the ACoS/TACoS distinction: ACoS is ad spend/ad-attributed sales; TACoS is total ad spend/total sales. The owner’s ~30% ACoS and ~10% TACoS are **business targets, not Amazon recommendations**; validate against contribution margin by SKU, market, VAT, shipping, and FBA/FBM costs. **Truth 9/10 for the definitions; 5/10 for universal suitability of those targets.**
- Do not infer organic-ranking causation from ad sales alone. Track organic sessions/orders/rank for the exact term, contribution margin, and inventory status over a consistent attribution window. Treat “ads cause ranking” as a test hypothesis, not a verified guarantee. **Truth 9/10 for the caution.**

### Contradictions / gaps

- **Direct contradiction:** owner strategy says `+` modifiers in broad campaigns generally; current Amazon help explicitly scopes broad-match modifiers to Sponsored Brands. This should be corrected before campaign build.
- Amazon’s public help does not promise that ads improve organic rank; secondary PPC practitioners often describe a rank lift. This is an attribution question requiring EWART data.
- No live campaign export, margins, bids, search-term report, placement data, or market-specific attribution was available. No bid or budget recommendation can be responsibly quantified.

## 2. Etsy listings

**Question:** What listing/search practices are safe to use across Etsy markets, given the Etsy connector is unavailable?

### Verified or cautiously reusable findings

1. Etsy’s published search guidance has historically described relevancy matching across titles, tags, categories, and attributes, plus listing quality/customer experience and personalization/context. The official article URL was reached but blocked by DataDome in this session, so the **current wording was not directly read**. **Truth 8/10** (official-source principle, but not current-page fetch).
2. The long-standing official listing structure includes 13 tags, relevant attributes/category, natural readable titles, complete descriptions/specifications, strong photos, and good customer-service/listing-quality signals. **Truth 8/10** for the durable structure; exact weighting is not verified and should not be presented as an algorithm formula.
3. Keyword stuffing, irrelevant tags, duplicate near-identical listings, and machine-translated phrases are poor-risk tactics. Local-language buyer phrasing should be researched separately for DE/FR/ES/LV/NO rather than copying US-EN keywords. **Truth 8/10** as best-practice guidance; no current Etsy weighting claim.

### Actionable EWART WOODS application

- Build a per-listing keyword map: product/material/style/use/room/occasion in the target market’s buyer language. Put the strongest natural phrase early in the title, but do not turn the title into a comma list.
- Fill every applicable category and attribute; use all 13 tags only when each is relevant and genuinely different. Preserve precise dimensions, wood type, finish, care, personalization, processing time, and shipping information.
- Use the owner’s existing search-term loop: keep terms that produce sales, negatively classify irrelevant high-spend terms, and propose title/tag fixes only after diagnosing why Etsy matched the query. Do not edit while the connector is unavailable.
- Improve the conversion evidence that Etsy’s search system can observe: accurate first photo, scale/context photo, material/finish close-up, dimensions, and honest delivery expectations. Test one material change at a time and compare impressions → listing views → orders.
- Avoid assuming that renewing a listing is a reliable ranking lever. If tested, record cost, impressions, visits, and orders; do not renew merely to “reset” SEO.

### Contradictions / gaps

- The farm skill says “first 40 characters most weighted,” “under ~140 chars,” and “renew/update regularly.” These are useful drafting heuristics but were not verified as current Etsy ranking rules in this session; rate them **5/10**, not policy.
- Etsy connector unavailable means no current titles/tags, search-term reports, listing stats, competitor sample, or market-specific query data could be checked.
- Etsy search is personalized/contextual; a single observed rank is not a stable universal position.

## 3. Amazon listings

**Question:** Which listing fundamentals and Amazon brand tools should EWART WOODS prioritize before increasing PPC?

### Verified/current findings

1. Amazon’s current public seller page presents **A+ Content, Brand Registry, Brand Analytics, Brand Store, Vine, and FBA** as brand/selling programs. This is directly visible on the live Amazon seller page, though eligibility varies by account/category/market. **Truth 9/10.**
2. A listing should make the product, material, dimensions, finish/variant, use, and delivery/fulfillment clear; the main image must represent the actual item and comply with the category’s image rules. The exact title character limits and image pixel/background requirements are category/market/account policy details and were not directly verified because Seller Central Help redirected to sign-in. **Truth 8/10 for the principle; 5/10 for any exact limit.**
3. A+ and Brand Store are not substitutes for a compliant, high-converting detail page. Use them to explain craftsmanship, scale, wood variation, care, comparison, and brand story; measure sales/conversion changes rather than assuming a fixed uplift. **Truth 9/10 for the operational principle; no uplift percentage asserted.**
4. For handmade wood decor, precise dimensions and contextual scale are high-priority conversion information. This is an EWART product/customer requirement, not a claim that Amazon gives a specific ranking boost. **Truth 9/10.**

### Actionable EWART WOODS application

- Audit top SKUs before adding spend: title clarity, browse node/category, bullets, backend search terms, variation/wood names, dimensions in both metric and market-appropriate units, care, production/fulfillment, and image compliance.
- Use an image sequence: compliant main image; size/scale against a known object; front/side/back; wood grain/finish; use context; packaging/care. Keep true product dimensions—never let a lifestyle render imply a different size.
- If eligible for Brand Registry, stage A+ modules and Brand Store pages around collections and use cases. Record SKU-level sessions, unit session percentage, ad sales, organic sales, and margin before/after. Do not promise a universal A+ conversion uplift.
- Localize vocabulary per marketplace (UK vs US spelling; DE/FR/ES/NO/LV phrasing). Do not translate wood species or care claims mechanically without a native-market check.
- Coordinate PPC with listing readiness: do not scale a keyword whose listing has weak main-image CTR or low conversion; fix the page first, then run a controlled ad test.

### Contradictions / gaps

- The farm strategy’s “advertise every product with a micro-campaign” is a test philosophy, not a requirement from Amazon. Budget should still be gated by margin, inventory, listing readiness, and measurable signal.
- Exact title limits, image rules, browse-node requirements, and A+ eligibility vary and require signed-in Seller Central/category checks. Do not hard-code the skill’s generic limits as policy.
- No EWART ASIN catalog, Brand Registry status, listing quality score, session/conversion data, review analysis, or inventory data was available.

## 4. Shopify

**Question:** What should EWART WOODS prioritize for organic discovery, conversion, and international markets on Shopify?

### Verified/current findings

1. Google’s current SEO Starter Guide says SEO helps search engines understand content and users find/make decisions; it explicitly warns there are no secrets that automatically rank a site first. Google recommends people-first useful content, descriptive organization/URLs, crawlable pages/resources, links, and (when useful) a sitemap; changes may take hours to months and should generally be assessed after a few weeks. **Truth 10/10** (directly fetched live Google page).
2. Shopify is a hosted platform, but the store still needs unique, descriptive product/collection titles and meta descriptions, useful visible copy, clear internal links, accurate image alt text, and clean canonical/indexing behavior. These are consistent with Shopify’s official SEO documentation, but Shopify Help returned a Cloudflare challenge in this session; exact current UI steps were not directly fetched. **Truth 8/10.**
3. International SEO is not “translate the same page and hope.” Market/language URLs, localized titles/descriptions, currency/shipping clarity, and correct language/alternate signals must be checked in the actual Shopify Markets configuration. **Truth 8/10** as a technical principle; implementation details depend on the plan/theme/domain setup.
4. Google Search Console/URL Inspection and GA4 are required for evidence: index coverage, queries, clicks, CTR, landing pages, add-to-cart, checkout, and purchase. Ranking claims without these are assumptions. **Truth 10/10.**

### Actionable EWART WOODS application

- Start with collection pages for commercial intent (e.g., handmade wooden home decor / wooden candle holders) and product pages for specific long-tail intent. Give each priority page a unique title, meta description, H1, intro, dimensions/material/care content, related-product links, and descriptive alt text.
- Use a canonical clean product URL; audit filter/sort/search URLs so they do not create indexable duplicate page sets. Verify with Search Console rather than assuming Shopify’s defaults are perfect for the installed theme/apps.
- Build localized market pages only where EWART can support accurate language, prices, VAT/shipping, returns, and customer service. Start with evidence-led markets; do not create thin machine-translated pages across all seven markets at once.
- Compress/size images and test mobile Core Web Vitals, but prioritize product clarity and conversion over chasing a single speed score. Track image CTR/engagement and product CVR after each controlled change.
- Add UTM links to external ads/blog placements and link blog content to relevant collections/products. Compare organic search, referral, and assisted revenue—not only last-click sales.

### Contradictions / gaps

- Shopify’s automatic sitemap/canonical behavior can be theme/app/URL dependent; “Shopify handles it” is not enough evidence for EWART’s live store.
- The skill’s exact meta lengths (~50–60 title, ~150–160 description) are drafting heuristics, not guaranteed Google display limits; Google rewrites snippets. Rate exact lengths **6/10**.
- No Shopify domain, theme, crawl, Search Console, GA4, product catalog, Markets configuration, or conversion data was provided; no live audit was performed.
