## 1. Reusable method — search-term classification (5 classes)

### 1.1 Inputs and windows

For every listing you need, per search term, per rolling window (30 and 90 days; weekly refresh):
impressions, clicks, ad spend, attributed orders, attributed revenue. Rules:

- **Minimum sample for a ROAS decision:** ≥ 30 clicks in 30 days, else term/listing is "learning —
  no verdict yet", never a scale decision.
- **Minimum window:** a 30-day window unless the product has been advertised < 6 weeks; then use
  whatever history exists and label the stage `NEW`.
- **Attribution:** take Etsy's reported ad-attributed orders/revenue as-is (Etsy applies its own
  attribution window). Only compare like-for-like windows.
- **Big spender** (must be explained, owner rule): top 10 terms of the listing by spend, or
  ≥ €15/30 days, or ≥ 10 × the listing's median term spend. Take the max.
- **Sales term:** ≥ 1 attributed order in the window.

### 1.2 Derived metrics (one formula block, applied to every term and every listing)

```
CTR   = clicks / impressions          AOV  = revenue / orders
CPC   = spend / clicks                ROAS_term = term revenue / term spend
CVR   = orders / clicks               ROAS_g  = listing revenue / listing spend (same window)
Break-even ROAS ≈ 1 / gross margin %  (€ profit per €1 revenue vs €1 ad cost — band in 1.4)
```

### 1.3 The decision table (class → rule → action → expected effect)

| Class | Decision rule (all AND-combined) | Action | Expected effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **KEEP** | relevant for this listing AND orders ≥ 1 AND sane economics (ROAS_term ≥ ~1.5 or CVR ≥ 10%) | No change; keep budget allowed | Traffic/profit continues |
| **ADD-NEGATIVE** | term irrelevant to THIS listing (different product, wrong material/animal/room, too generic) AND spends ≥ €3 OR ≥ 5 clicks | Add the term as negative (Etsy: search terms → add negative); no listing edit | Ad stops showing on that query; wasteful spend stops (save = current spend) |
| **FIX-IN-LISTING** | term does NOT match the product BUT is explained by title/tag/attribute content (owner rule: diagnose WHY it shows — title? tags? mismatched attribute?) | (a) Remove the driver from title/tags/attributes; (b) ALSO add the term as negative so it stops appearing; both pending | Mismatch spend stops at the SOURCE (permanently, incl. organic), not just per ad |
| **PROMOTE-TO-LISTING** | term relevant + has sales (≥ 1 order) + NOT yet in title/tags | Add the exact phrase to the 13 tags; if strong (≥ 3 orders or ROAS_term ≥ 3), also add to the title (middle position) | Listing gains relevance for that query → organic + ad expansion of a proven term |
| **PROMOTE-TO-FRONT** | term has sales + HIGH VOLUME (≥ 3 orders OR top decile by spend) | Put the phrase at the VERY START of the title (first ~40 chars), keep ≤ 140 total; owner rule: strongest terms in front | Position rises on the term that actually converts → more sales per same ad spend |

**Weekly loop priority (owner rule):** 1) close the irrelevant terms (negative), 2) for every big
spender that doesn't match — diagnose WHY it shows (title? tags? attribute?) and remove the source
from the listing, 3) add proven sale terms into the listing, 4) front-of-title the strongest ones.

### 1.4 Bid decision — the owner's 3-bid-variant framework, operationalized

Owner rule (`etsy-ads-search-terms.md`): *"three bid/placement variants; new product → lower bid,
test, expect worse ROAS first; product running a while → use ROAS history to choose the right
variant; scale the one that is profitable. ROAS is the decision metric."* Mapping to three named
variants (framework — live dashboard labels verified at the first pull, §6.4):

| Variant | Name | When (stage × ROAS) | Typical bid setting | Rationale (owner framework) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — TEST (low)** | Learn | Stage **NEW** (advertised < 6–8 wks OR < 30 clicks) whatever the ROAS | Lowest of the 3 levels (e.g., suggested-bid × ~0.7) | New product → data at low risk; expect worse ROAS first (owner) |
| **A — TEST (low)** | Reduce | Stage RUNNING AND ROAS < break-even | Lowest level, only while terms/listing are being fixed | Don't scale a loss; fix terms + listing first, re-run in 2 weeks |
| **B — MAINTAIN (mid)** | Hold | Stage RUNNING AND break-even ≤ ROAS < 1.5 × break-even | Mid level (suggested bid × ~1.0) | Proven steady profit = the majority case for healthy products |
| **C — SCALE (high)** | Scale | Stage RUNNING AND ROAS ≥ 1.5 × break-even | Highest level (bid × ~1.2–1.35) | profitable → push harder, buy position; ROAS may dip, re-check next cycle |

**The uncomplicated check (one line per product):**

```
stage flag = NEW if (weeks advertised < 6  OR  clicks_30d < 30)  else RUNNING
ROAS_W = revenue_attributed(30d) / ad_spend(30d)
IF stage NEW            → A (test-low; log CTR + CPC trend, do NOT judge ROAS yet)
ELSE IF ROAS_W ≥ 3.0    → C (SCALE)
ELSE IF ROAS_W ≥ 2.0    → B (MAINTAIN)
ELSE (ROAS_W < 2.0)     → A-reduce + fix terms/listing first; re-run in 2 weeks
```

**Bands — ASSUMPTION (needs owner sign-off):** no numeric Etsy ROAS target exists in the owner doc.
Proposal: break-even ROAS ≈ **2.0** (assumes ≈ 50% gross margin on our products; at margin `m`,
break-even = 1/m) → B-band 2.0–2.99, C-band **≥ 3.0**. Bid multipliers (×0.7 / ×1.0 / ×1.2) are
adjustable defaults. Flagged `[assumption]` wherever used.
