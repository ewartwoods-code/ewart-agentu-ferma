### 4. Quick wins — PROPOSALS ONLY, nothing applied

1. **Stop the BE sprawl.** Amazon.com.be runs **110 campaigns** for one order and 111.3% ACoS; 65 of them spent money and sold nothing (€55.02). Proposal: pause every BE campaign except `C-SP | TP Wave | auto` (the only one that sold — ROAS 17.71 on €2.94), then rebuild BE as 2–3 campaigns. **Recovers ~€55 per 30 days** and removes 65 objects from every future report.
2. **Pause the FR Sponsored Brands campaign.** `SB_Floating Shelves_store page/vibe video/exact kw` is the single largest zero-sale burn in the account: €47.88, 274 clicks, no orders, still enabled. Proposal: pause it. **Recovers ~€48 per 30 days.** (Pausing works via the API; SB bid and placement tuning would be console-only.)
3. **Cut the DE zero-sale cluster.** Four *enabled* DE campaigns spent €62.59 with 501 dead clicks and no sales: `C-SP | TP Honey | broad 1` (€23.79), `SP Sofarm trays | manual exact` (€16.07), `C-SP | TP Cloud | auto` (€11.88), `C-SP | TP Cloud | phrase 1` (€10.85). Proposal: pull the search-term report first, add phrase negatives on the shared dead stems, and pause whatever still has >50 clicks with no order. **Recovers up to ~€63 per 30 days.**
4. **Bid down the one over-target seller.** `SP_TP Wave_FBA_broad` (DE) at 99.9% ACoS falls in the ≥40% band → **−20% on keyword bids**, not a pause: it does convert, just not at a price that works. Live bids must be read with `list_sp_keywords` before proposing exact values.
5. **Move money to UK and NL.** UK sits at 12.7% ACoS and NL at 8.2%, both far under the 30% target, on £83.50 and €26.36 for the entire month. Proposal: raise daily budgets on 3.11, 3.12 and 3.5 and check whether they are budget-capped. This is the only proposal here that *adds* spend, and it is the one most likely to add profit.
6. **Decide on IT and SE.** IT spent €21.90 and SE 35.29 SEK for zero sales. Proposal: either pause both, or keep one discovery campaign each at a €3/day cap and judge again in 30 days. Doing nothing is the expensive option.

Combined effect of proposals 1–4 and 6: roughly **€190 per 30 days** of spend redirected away from traffic that is currently buying nothing, plus 35.29 SEK.
