## 2. Deliverable 2 — Region-fit logic

### 2.1 Which regions (evidence, not priors)

Measured EWART WOODS facts (this farm's own data, t-0008/persona t-0031):

- **Amazon = 97% of measured revenue**, AOV €75, buyers on DE list in German, FR in French.
- **Ad spend 30d:** FR €630.88, DE €608.76, UK €83.50, BE €57.96, SE €35.29, NL €26.36, ES €26.04, IT €21.90, PL/Ie €0.
- Top organic sellers: German- and French-titled bathroom/shelf items → **DE and FR are the measured #1/#2**.
- US-EN is described by persona as the biggest **Etsy** decor audience, but no US revenue is measured in our data yet → treat as *opportunity*, not proof.
- LV = home market (Shopify/brand proof), tiny B2C.

**Decision rule (region ranking for blog hunting, weekly, refreshed when t-0034 adds geo):**
score(region) = 0.45 × measured revenue share + 0.30 × blog-market size (German/French interior blog scene is dense and guest-post friendly) + 0.15 × placement budget-fit + 0.10 × language match of our catalog (DE/FR titles already exist).

Order today: **DE → FR → UK → ES/IT/NL/SE/BE → US (Etsy) → LV/NO** .

### 2.2 Blog fit criteria — the 5 gates (all must pass)

1. **Niche (home-decor/interior/blogs about rooms)**: floating shelves, bathroom storage, wall decor, plant styling, wooden furniture, scandi/modern — overlaps our catalog (206 products, 117 wall/shelf lines).
2. **Audience match**: reads like our persona (woman 25–49; small flats; plants; dog; kids/Montessori; gifting) — check the blog's own photo/author voice.
3. **Region**: the blog's audience is IN the region we pick (SimilarWeb geo split / DE language / local city references).
4. **Active**: posted within last ~3 months (we prove via RSS/WP dates; a blog dormant since 2020 cannot be paid).
5. **Traffic/engagement proof**: est. reach with source + truth 1–10; no number = no purchase above ~0 until verified.
6. (0) Commonsense: alive, visible Impressum/Kontakt, no weird redirect/brand-squat (we verify each domain's current identity — e.g. sodapop-design.de is now a casino spam site!).

**Scoring rubric (feeds the owner's 1–10 rating):** niche 25p + audience 20p + region 15p + activity 20p + traffic/engagement proof 20p = 100 → scale to 10. Combine with cost via (rating × reach)/cost (owner rule).

### 3.1 Sources — repeatable discovery (all free, no auth)

### 3.2 Fields to extract (per candidate)

`blog name | url | feed_url | region | language | niche | contact (email/form/IG) | subscriber signals (RSS count, newsletter) | last 5 post dates | post frequency | social follow counts (if visible) | reach claims (on-site) | traffic_est (with source+truth 1–10) | notes/flags (dormant, cert errors, brand hijack)`

### 3.3 data/blogs.csv schema (exactly as required)

```
blog, url, region, language, niche, contact, traffic_source, traffic_est, notes
```

- `contact`: best-available on-site contact — email if in an Impressum/Kontakt, else "Kontaktformular/<URL>" or IG handle + the page it was read from.
- `traffic_source`: what the estimate is based on (self-report, activity, SimilarWeb [blocked]...).
- `traffic_est`: a range/label **explicitly flagged** `[est. unsourced]` or with the measured source; numbers only when they came from a real fetch + truth rating in `notes`.
- `notes`: truth 1–10 for the estimate, freshness evidence (last post date + where found), flags (dormant / cert / pivoted niche).
