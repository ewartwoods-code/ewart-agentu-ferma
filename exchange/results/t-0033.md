Owner rules (from `knowledge/owner-strategy/blog-placement-strategy.md`): weekly contact of found

## 1. Outreach workflow spec

Full spec: **`.claude/agents/outreach-agent.md`** (agent registry note in `AGENTS.md`, §13).
Highlights:

- **Weekly selection (Monday):** from `data/blogs.csv` (t-0030 feeds it; first run seeds from this
  file). Filter `status∈(new,contacted) & rating≥5 & region∈(DE,FR,LV,EU,WW)`; score
  `value = (rating × reach)/cost_cap` (conservative & optimistic passes when reach is unsourced);
  pick 8–10 candidates, ≥2 per target region; forced no-go on ask >€100 unless owner pre-approves.
- **Personalization gate** (§4): every email must (1) open with one real, recent article of the
  blog (verbatim title/summary in their language), (2) carry ONE product story (Baltic wood, ≤120 cm,
  oak/ash/walnut/wenge/cherry, Fuji-style photos; product matched to the blog's last articles),
  (3) offer a format menu (article / product test / listicle / styled shoot), (4) one CTA, ≤180
  words, disclosure-ready (Anzeige/Publicité/Reklāma).
- **Negotiation vs stats/rating** (§5): anchor on (rating×reach)/€; cap table rating 5–6 → €30–60,
  7–8 → €60–90, 9–10 → €90–100 (€100+ owner-gated); prefer value packages over price cuts (2-post
  bundle, newsletter/social add-on, ≥90-day evergreen slot, dofollow backlink, we deliver finished
  DE/FR/LV text + photos, we share UTM results); demand proof of stats above €60; log every price talk.
- **Follow-up cadence** (§6): day 0 contact → day 4 (F1, ≤80 words) → day 9 (F2, add new value) →
  day 14 (F3 close-or-park) → day 21 park 90 days. ≥8 active conversations maintained until
  2 placements/week are rolling. Max 3 follow-ups.
- **Post-publication tracking** (§7): UTM `utm_source=blog:<slug>&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=
  outreach-<YYYY-WW>&utm_content=<product>`; checkpoints +1/+7/+30 days (GA4/Shopify/Etsy/Amazon
  sessions, visitors, add-to-cart, orders, revenue); ROI = (revenue−price)/price; monthly rating
  recompute incl. the new `rating_past` component; one-paragraph buy-again verdict per blog;
  quarterly pool re-rank.
- **Safety:** research/draft/track auto; send/money/publish = approval. No fabricated data:
  unsourced fields stay `[unsourced]`.

> month ({{Month}} openings) at your standard rate? Thank you either way.

**Language notes (per region — DE / FR / LV):**

- **DE (Germany):**
  - Formal: "Sie/Ihnen/Ihre"; individual bloggers often use du — mirror the blog's own voice
    (check their page; if they address readers with "du", use du from message 2 on).
  - Labeling is a legal must: sponsored posts need a visible mark („Werbung", „Anzeige",
    "bezahlte Kooperation"); we supply the label String.
  - Keep prices in € incl. stated VAT/payment terms; typical small-blog rate: €30–€100;
    portals (e.g. Freshideen tier) €120–€500+ — the cap table in §5 applies.
  - Subject lines: lowercase is normal ("eine deko-story für wohnklamotte"), but our templates
    use initial capital (fine); marketing-y words reduced — German readers are anti-spam-sensitive.
- **FR (France):**
  - Default "vous" (vouvoiement) for any site/magazine (e.g. Joli Place — "Votre magazine");
    switch to "tu" only if the blog talks to readers as "tu" AND you've had one reply.
  - Disclosure per ARPP/DDADUE: "Publicité", "Article sponsorisé" or "Partenariat rémunéré".
  - Tone: polite + warm ("Je me permets de vous écrire…"), rates €60–€250 for niche déco;
    many accept product-in-kind (écrire "produits offerts + lien").
  - Mention GDPR: no attachments, clear opt-in; privacy policy page link.
- **LV (Latvia):**
  - Market is small: few LV interior blogs; most "influence" is Instagram/TikTok — budget
    €20–€60 or product gifting; для нас the LV blog channel is mostly for local PR/brand, not
    big traffic.
  - Formal "Jūs" for portals; "tu" after first contact for personal bloggers.
  - Disclosure: "Reklāmraksts" / "Reklāma" (Patērētāju tiesību aizsardzības centrs expectations).
  - Media: use Latvian topics (oak, wenge, Baltic pine, hand-crafted), cool language.
  - LV is a nice "home turf" signal for EU craft — small but high-trust.
