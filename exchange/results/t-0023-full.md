## Product concept

Wall-mounted coat rack: a single solid-oak backboard with 4 turned/rounded hooks, warm
"Fujifilm"-grade natural look — fits the brand (Baltic wood home decor, oak/ash/walnut/wenge/
cherry, ≤ 120 cm, made in Latvia).

## Dimensioned design (finished product)

- Backboard: **80 cm wide × 12 cm tall × 2 cm thick** (solid oak, one piece).
- Hooks: 4, Ø 3 cm, protruding **8 cm** from the board, spaced evenly (16 cm apart, centre-to-centre).
- Finished **longest side = 80 cm ≤ 120 cm** ✅.
- Packed (box): **86 × 20 × 16 cm** → longest side **86 cm ≤ 120 cm** ✅.
- Fixings: 2 keyhole hangers on the back (+ screws/wall anchors, shown as "supplied" optional).
- Finish: natural oil, satin.

## Material list / recipe

- Solid oak, one board 82 × 14 × 4.2 cm (cut to 80×12, planed to 2 cm) — approx. 0.6 kg.
- 4 × oak dowel/blank Ø3 × 8 cm for hooks.
- PVA wood glue; 2 × keyhole hangers + screws (+ 4 wall anchors, optional).
- Natural satin wood oil (±30 ml).
- Packaging: cardboard box 86 × 20 × 16 cm + corner pads.
