## 0. Owner FBA strategy — applied rules (knowledge/owner-strategy/fba-stock-strategy.md)

These are the operational rules the weekly restock analysis must implement:

### 0.1 Send groups (every product in the proposal is tagged with one)
| Group | Definition (owner) | Restock logic |
|---|---|---|
| **Proven** | Sells fast in the warehouse with good profit | Send the batch size the ramp says; priority `now` when days-of-supply (DoS) < reorder point |
| **Test** | Never sent to FBA before but shows potential from other-channel stats (own warehouse/Shopify/Etsy) | **Send exactly 5 pcs** first; if they sell → next batch larger, then larger again (ramp step-by-step) |
| **Old** | Sent before, but has not been in the warehouse for a long time | Re-check carefully before sending; if the listing is stale or the market moved → treat as test with 5-pc start or do not send |

2. **Never exceed 60 days of stock in the warehouse** — hard cap. Anything that would push coverage beyond 60 days of velocity must NOT be shipped in that batch (defer or split).
3. **ROI on shipping**: we ship by **AIR with FedEx, up to 100 kg per shipment** — fast delivery lets us rotate stock quickly and avoid storage fees. The send-size logic therefore targets quick turns (≤60 days) and small frequent batches; if a single proposal would exceed ~100 kg, split into two air shipments rather than one large one.
4. **Slow movers** (stock long in warehouse): must be **sold off fastest** — discounts / Lightning Deals; anything sitting in the warehouse must also have **ACTIVE ads** for visibility and max profit. Slow movers are excluded from restock until their sell-through recovers.
5. **Wood-variation ratio** (products with 5 wood variations: **oak / ash / wenge / walnut / cherry**, owner list): ratio is calculated per family from **historical sales per variation** — best-selling woods ship in LARGER volume, slower woods smaller. Send qty per variation = `round(r_i × family_send_qty)`, r_i = share of that variation in historical sales. Ratio is re-computed as data grows (monthly minimum).

Proposed default thresholds (config, pending owner/Signie confirmation): safety stock 14 days; lead time = production 21 days until actual production lead times per family are supplied; FedEx air transit 4–6 days; FBA intake 3–7 days.

## 1. FBA restock analysis spec — inputs and formulas

For every FBA candidate ASIN:

| Input | Definition | Source | Status in repo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales velocity `v` | units sold per day = Σ units in window / window days. Windows: 90-day primary, 30-day minimum; holiday period optional (Nov-Dec) | Amazon Sales export | **MISSING** |
| Stock on hand | FBA **available sellable** quantity (per fulfillment center summed) | FBA Inventory export (`quantity available`) | **MISSING** |
| Inbound | qty in transit to FBA (not yet available) | FBA All Listings (`quantity inbound`) | **MISSING** |
| Days of supply (DoS) | `(available + inbound) / v` | calc | pending inputs |
| Lead time | production (Signie) + packing + FedEx air transit + FBA intake | default 21+5+4 ≈ 30 days until confirmed | default config |
| Seasonality / trend | same-week last year + rolling 8-week trend; ad-spend events (Lightning Deal, seasonal) shift `v` | factor applied to `v` | **MISSING** (needs Sales history) |
| Sell-through | units sold last 30 d / (available + inbound) — low = slow mover | threshold e.g. < 20% flags liquidation list | pending inputs |
| Safety stock | `v × safety_days` (default 14 d) | pending inputs |
| Reorder point | `v × (lead_time + review_period 7d) + safety_stock` | pending inputs |
| Candidate | DoS < reorder point ⇒ propose restock; DoS > 60 d ⇒ do NOT send (cap); slow-mover flag ⇒ exclude + liquidate | |
| Send qty | clamp `v × 60` − (available + inbound) up to batch size; min 5 for test group; ramp: 5 → 2×5 → ~15 → ~30 while selling | |

**Worked example of the math (ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY — placeholder numbers, NOT real data):**
ASIN X: available 6, inbound 0, 90-day units 50 → `v = 0.556`/day. DoS = 6/0.556 ≈ 10.8 d. Lead 30 d + 7 d review → reorder point = 0.556 × 37 ≈ 20.6 ⇒ 6 < 21 → restock. Cap: 0.556 × 60 ≈ 33.3 ⇒ send up to 33 − 6 = 27 pcs (round to pack multiple). If ASIN is in group test → send 5 pcs only, re-evaluate next week.
