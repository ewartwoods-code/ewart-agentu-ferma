## 1. Review-mining method (repeatable)

Used the `marketplace-research` skill plus its `review-mining-evidence-coding` reference. Summary of the process:

1. **Fix the lane.** For each category we already sell, choose one query family on one marketplace (Amazon) and a hard ≤120 cm finished-size cap. Do not mix materially different products into one ranking.
2. **Scan the market structure.** Collect the top ~40 organic results per query: brand, ASIN, rating, rating count, "X+ bought in past month" badge, Small Business badge, price. Treat rating/review totals as **cumulative, lagging** and the purchase badge as a **medium-strength demand proxy** — never convert either to sales volume.
3. **Pull verbatim reviews.** For 6 selected products (5 competitors + 1 of our own listings), read the **top-review block rendered on each product detail page** and copy full review text (title, star count, market/date, body) verbatim. This is a *listing-scan + review-text corpus* at the DP level, not the whole review feed.
4. **Code themes.** Tag each readable review by sentiment (positive / negative / wished-missing) and label the theme (e.g. "material honesty", "size accuracy", "stability"). **Counts below are counts within the readable sample**, with the product review total shown for context. This follows the evidence-coding rule: never present a sample count as if it covered the full review set, and never turn a single anecdote into "customers want X".
5. **Translate gaps into hypotheses.** Every opportunity is coded as `observed signal → confidence → proposed feature → validation test`, never as a fabricated direct customer wish.

**Bloom of the method:** works with any browser-capable node; Amazon DP top-review blocks are server-rendered so no login is needed. Etsy is DataDome-blocked here → repeat on Etsy manually before launch.

**Synthesis (directional, not audited market-wide demand):** across the five competitors, the two largest *negative* clusters are (a) **dimension inaccuracy** — the item arrives smaller/thinner than advertised — and (b) **material dishonesty** — "wood" that is actually particle board / printed or pressed material. A credible, **true-to-size, solid-wood** brand that also fixes stability at height wraps around the biggest expressed wishes.

- **Next / go-no-go:** prototype the middle height; load test 8 kg at full height with no tip; interview 5 buyers on the price point (~EUR30–55 estimate).

**All three respect the owner's ≤120 cm rule, reuse the existing 5-wood system and workshop skill set, and each closes a distinct expressed gap: material honesty + true size (1), adult leg clearance + rigid hinge (2), stability at height (3).**

## 8. Latvian owner summary (for the owner, short & plain)

No mēs izlasījām konkurentu atsauksmes Amazon (5 produktus: plaukti, tualetes papīra turētājs, augu stends, gultas paplāte). Cilvēkiem patīk viegla uzstādīšana un izskats, bet viņi sūdzas: **"koks" bieži ir mākslīgs/plāns, izmēri neatbilst solītajam** un **statīvi šūpojas lielā augstumā**.

Mūsu stils (no kataloga): **īsts koks** (ozols, osis, valrieksts, venge, ķirsis) + metāls, nordic/skandināvu un viļņotas "organiskas" formas.

2. **Gultas/galda paplāte** ar augstākām kājām (lai var ielikt kājas) un stiprām, nelūstošām eņģēm.
3. **Stabils koka augu stends/pjedestāls** (bez šūpošanās lielā augstumā, bez slīdošām kājām, kas lūst).
