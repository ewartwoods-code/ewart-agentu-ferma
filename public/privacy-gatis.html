# EWART Gatis Bridge — Privacy Policy **Effective date: 2026-08-17** ## What this service is This is the task bridge between the EWART WOODS business system (Hermes) and the owner's personal GPT assistant (Gatis). It runs at `https://ewart-agentu-ferma-production.up.railway.app` and is operated by EWART WOODS (SIA, Latvia) for its own internal use. ## What data is handled The bridge transfers **business task data only**: - task text (what the assistant was asked to work on); - the result summary and its status (done / blocked / needs review); - optional reference to the shared farm context document; - optional learned-skill notes that the assistant saves. This data comes from and belongs to the **owner of EWART WOODS** (the business data of the EWART WOODS stores). Examples: product names, prices, marketing texts, order summaries. The bridge is **not** a public service — it is used exclusively by the owner through their own GPT subscription account. ## What data is NOT used - No customer personal data is collected, stored, or shared. - No payment or bank data. - No cookies, no trackers, no analytics, no ads, no third-party shares. - The bridge does not sell or rent any data. ## Storage and security - Data is stored in the EWART WOODS private GitHub repositories (ewartwoods-code/ewart-woods-farm) and the Railway-hosted application, which the owner controls. - Access to the API requires the owner's own actions/credentials. The endpoints have an activity and are only reachable through the approved action configuration. - Task/results are kept until the owner intentionally deletes them. ## Contact Questions about this policy: ewartwoods@gmail.com (owner, EWART WOODS). ## Changes If anything changes in data handling, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
